Use the [product guide](../../product/Home.md) for current behavior and the [maintenance guide](../../maintenance.md) for current contributor requirements.

## Finalization that supersedes the retained handoff status

On September 22, 2026, independent controller verification finalized S08 as **Done / Proven / On scope / Stop confirmed Yes** and closed Project #25. The archived README, disposition index, and completion ledger predate that finalization and retain their original Partial/open observations.

[Independent finalization record](https://github.com/mbh-solutions/supportability-gate/issues/209#issuecomment-5781049063). The finalization records ten restored qualification/live bundles and distinguishes clean profile qualification from failing consumer baselines. It does not convert every enrolled consumer result into PASS.

Later protected maintenance advanced the deployed workflow beyond that qualification SHA. Historical pins and run results are dated observations, not current deployment instructions.
